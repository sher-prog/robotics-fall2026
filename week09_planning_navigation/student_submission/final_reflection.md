Respond to any or all of these prompts:

2. How did this activity affect your motivation to do similar kinds of work in the future?
3. What value do you see in connecting technical or computing work with human, ethical, or societal considerations?

## Response

This lab showed me how much work goes into a robot just getting from one place to another. I liked writing the search code and then watching the robot drive the path in Gazebo. It surprised me that a path that looked fine on the grid, like BFS on the constrained map, could still fail when the robot drove it. That made me more interested in this field.

The part that stood out most was the human-aware navigation. A route can be the shortest and still pass too close to a person. So adding a personal-space field made the robot keep more distance for almost no extra time, but one setting also got stuck and failed. It showed me that a robot working near people has to be safe and comfortable for them, not only fast.

I think it is important to connect technical work with human and ethical concerns. These tests used fixed people, but real people move and react, so we can't just assume a robot is safe. This lab made me want to do more projects like this, and to test carefully before trusting the results.
