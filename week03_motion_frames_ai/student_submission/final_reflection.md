## Response

This activity really highlighted how software development and physical robotics intersect, especially when transitioning from clean simulation environments to real-world spaces. Working on motion frames and AI-assisted ROS development made me think deeply about how technical precision is only one half of engineering; code that is mathematically correct in a vacuum can still fail if it ignores human and societal contexts. What stood out to me most was how easy it is to rely on simulation metrics like trajectory tolerances while completely missing real-world safety challenges, such as how pedestrians actually navigate hallways. Connecting computing work with ethical and human considerations adds crucial depth, showing that engineers must account for accessibility, unpredictability, and shared spaces right from the design stage. This experience has strengthened my motivation to build intelligent systems thats safe and robust for everyday human environments.
